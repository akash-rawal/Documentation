Here's what's new.

Previously, users could only be added to a single annotation project at a time. Now you can manage users at the workspace level, where each workspace consists of multiple ongoing annotation projects.

**Key Capabilities:**
- **Workspace-Level Access:** Add and manage users once at the workspace level instead of per project.
- **Project-Wide Coverage:** New users are available across all annotation projects within the workspace.
- **Simplified Onboarding:** Onboard annotators, reviewers, and managers without repeating project-by-project setup.

Previously, each annotated object in a video had its own separate timeline. Now there is a single timeline on which every object is overlaid, making it easy for annotators to see all annotation segments at a glance.

**Key Capabilities:**
- **Single Overlay Timeline:** All annotated objects are displayed on one timeline.
- **Segment Visibility:** See how long each annotation segment is and where it occurs.
- **Easier Video QA:** Compare and review object segments without switching between timelines.

## 5. Lag-Free Video Streaming

Video annotation used to suffer from latency and slow playback while streaming frames. Video streaming has been fully optimized, eliminating playback lag when annotating frames.

**Key Capabilities:**
- **Instant Playback:** No more latency when playing videos during annotation.
- **Smooth Frame Scrubbing:** Navigate and annotate frames without buffering delays.
- **Faster Workflows:** Spend time annotating instead of waiting for video to load.

---

## 6. Hear Audio While Annotating Videos

Previously, audio was stripped from videos during dataset creation, leaving annotators with silent footage. Video audio is now retained and can be heard while annotating.

**Key Capabilities:**
- **Audio Retention:** Video files keep their audio track through dataset creation.
- **In-Player Playback:** Listen to audio directly in the annotation player for richer context.
- **Better Annotation Context:** Use audio cues to make more accurate decisions while labeling.

## Why It Matters

These updates focus on three key pillars of annotation efficiency:

1. **Streamlined Administration:** Manage users at the workspace level and onboard teams faster across projects.
2. **Better Video Annotation Experience:** Retained audio, latency-free streaming, and a unified timeline make video labeling faster and more accurate.
3. **Enterprise-Grade Security:** SSO enforcement and IP allowlisting give you control over who can access your workspace.

Together, these enhancements help computer vision teams accelerate dataset creation while maintaining the highest quality and security standards.

## FAQs

**Q1. Can I still add users to individual projects?**
Yes, project-level user management remains available. Workspace-level management simply adds a faster way to onboard users across multiple projects at once.

**Q2. Which identity providers are supported for SSO enforcement?**
Google Workspace and Okta are currently supported.

**Q3. How does the unified timeline help with video QA?**
All annotated objects are overlaid on a single timeline, so reviewers can instantly see where and how long each annotation segment is, without toggling between multiple per-object timelines.
